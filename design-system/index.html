Visual language

A graphite field, islands for floating controls, Geist for the interface and IBM Plex Mono for values. One signal accent, reserved for live and focused things; drawn as a line it uses the stroke role so it stays readable on Paper. Every state is a dot and a word. Both themes share every role; text, supporting and metadata text and the state words meet WCAG AA on every surface, and lines and rings meet 3:1.

Where things live

  • design.md: the specification — model, principles, layout, integrity rules, and the extension checklist.
  • tokens.css: every colour, type, spacing, radius, shadow, layer and motion value, for Graphite and Paper, including the terminal palette.
  • apps/desktop/src/renderer/ui/kit.tsx: the primitives, with one state vocabulary (ui/vocabulary.ts) and one formatter for quantities and times (ui/format.ts).
  • The in-app Design system view (launcher footer or ⌘K): every primitive, rendered from the real component, in both themes side by side, with measured contrast.
